# Source provenance

The paper snapshots, PDFs, extracted full texts, QMD database and embeddings remain in the original lattice-work workspace. They are **not bundled in these forks**. Links from research notes that formerly targeted a local paper now lead to the corresponding provenance entry here, not to a redistributed paper body.

The SHA-256 values below identify the actual parsed Markdown bytes used locally. They do not establish author authenticity, theorem correctness, reading coverage, redistribution permission or security admission. QMD paths identify the indexed source; public locators identify related publications, not a claim that their current revisions are byte-equivalent to the local source. The companion reading ledgers record coverage and OCR/image limitations.
