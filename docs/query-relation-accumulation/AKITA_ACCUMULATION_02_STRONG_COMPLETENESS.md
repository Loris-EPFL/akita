# Obligation 02: all-valid-state strong completeness and source-domain admission

**Status: research proposal, not an implementation or an established Akita accumulation theorem.** The preferred domain certificate below is a complete deterministic construction. The witness-update algebra and worst-case admission bounds are proved under explicit profile conditions. Existence of a secure fixed-capacity receiving profile and a modular backward extractor remain separate open obligations. No branch or implementation changes are proposed here.

## 1. Success criterion and the distinction that matters

An accumulator may be provided by a mutually distrustful party. Completeness must therefore quantify over **every decider-valid state**, not only states generated by a particular honest history. A weak source recovered by knowledge extraction need not belong to the honest short-source domain. These are three different assertions:

1. **Honest-history completeness:** a prover beginning with canonical commitments succeeds along its own histories, possibly with abort probability.
2. **All-valid-state completeness:** every admitted old state with a valid witness can be updated with every admitted new input, including noncanonical but relation-valid decommitments.
3. **Backward knowledge soundness:** an accepted update and a valid successor yield matching predecessor/input witnesses, or a priced security failure.

Neither (1) nor (3) automatically implies (2). Computational uniqueness cannot substitute for a universal honest-update algorithm.

The published [Halo Infinite, Definition 6.1 and its private-accumulation paragraph](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-030-84242-0_23.pdf), CRYPTO 2021, pp. 675–676, quantifies correctness over inputs whose original verifiers and old deciders accept. Private accumulation additionally gives the prover opening states. The construction here follows that quantifier, while explicitly accounting for the larger private witness relation and possible sampler aborts. An existentially valid commitment without an available opening witness is not sufficient input to a private prover.

### Quantified native statement

Fix a public profile $P$ and public parameters $\mathsf{pp}$ satisfying the admission conditions in Sections 3–6. For all $t<T_{\max}$, all typed states $a$, all witnesses $\omega$, all typed fresh batches $u$ and witnesses $\xi$ satisfying

$$
R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,a;\omega)=1,
\qquad R_{\mathrm{in}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,u;\xi)=1,
\qquad \mathsf{Link}(P,a,u)=1,
$$

run the specified honest update on these very witnesses, not replacement canonical commitments:

$$
(a',\omega',\pi)\leftarrow
\mathsf{Update}(P,a,\omega,u,\xi;\rho).
$$

The claimed **conditional all-valid-state completeness theorem** is

$$
\Pr_\rho\left[
\mathsf{VerifyUpdate}(P,a,u,a',\pi)=1
\ \land\ R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,a';\omega')=1
\ \land\ \mathsf{Dec}_{\mathrm{reveal}}(P,a',\omega')=1
\right]\geq 1-\varepsilon_{\mathrm{adm}}(P).
\tag{SC}
$$

Its hypotheses are deterministic range/shape coverage for the **entire declared relations**, exact reduction correctness, and the actual challenge-sampling rules. If sampling never fails and there is no tensor-factor rejection or proof-of-work search, $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{adm}}=0$: the algebra has perfect completeness. This is not a claim that the existing optimized Akita schedules meet those hypotheses. For a public decider, regard the supplied final certificate $\omega'$ as part of the final discharged accumulator; before discharge it remains the prover's private opening state. No knowledge or SIS assumption is used to prove (SC).

The time/count restriction $t<T_{\max}$ is a public relation restriction, not a claim of completeness at an exhausted state. A state at $T_{\max}$ is terminal and may be discharged, but is not an admitted update predecessor.

## 2. What the source papers actually establish

### 2.1 Akita: decommitment is not re-running Commit

[Akita](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita), indexed local version, §10.2, Definitions 10.13–10.14, qmd `line:3603-3990`:

- Definition 10.13 authenticates source blocks through $A$, inner-image digits, the exact outer slices $B$, and the active $F$ chain. It decodes **full commitment-grid vectors**. It requires digit alphabets on the carried image/compression digits, but neither canonical gadget source decomposition nor membership in the honest padding image. Algebraic-padding cells have no implicit zero condition.
- Definition 10.14 additionally has units $\bar c$ with bounds on $\iota(\bar c)s$, not automatically on $s$. Partial evaluations and the shared $D/H$ path are opening-specific, not part of the commitment-side source projection.
- Equation (202) proves decoded full-grid evaluations. Natural-domain padded-vector semantics require a separate padding-image predicate, or an application-specific restriction argument.

Akita §10.2, Remark 10.32, qmd `line:4141-4250`, expressly declines canonical/short-source extraction. The source after challenge subtraction contains an inverse unit difference. Strong–weak composition does not make inversion norm-preserving. The same section distinguishes the classical ROM proof from quantum attack-cost sizing; it does not supply a QROM accumulation theorem.

Akita §5.4, qmd `line:2307-2376`, separates:

$$
T_f=\left(\frac b2-1\right)\frac{b^{\delta_f}-1}{b-1}
\quad\text{(positive canonical encoding capacity)},
$$

$$
\beta_f^{\mathrm{cert}}=\frac{b^\star}{2}\frac{b^{\delta_f}-1}{b-1}
\quad\text{(every accepted recomposed response bound)}.
$$

Using the second as if it were the first is a completeness error. Equation (109) also includes quotient/compression digits and specified alignment/ring-completion/Boolean-domain zeros; none may disappear when defining the receiver.

### 2.2 Akita completeness can abort and is history-conditioned

Akita §10.1, qmd `line:3453-3602`, Definitions 10.5–10.8 and Theorem 10.9, accounts for finite sampler trials, response encoding/norm rejection, tensor-factor zeros, and proof-of-work exhaustion. Its response-model premise is pointwise over reachable honest prefixes. An average success estimate does not justify raising failure probability to a retry count: retries reuse the same fixed source. This is still not a uniform theorem over every source admitted by a newly declared accumulation decider.

The useful repair is Akita Appendix G.2, Corollary G.6, qmd `line:6133-6216`: deterministic source and challenge envelopes cover every source prefix without assuming independent/uniform recursive digits. This document applies its elementary inequalities to the stronger relation domain, rather than importing the optimized recursive response model.

### 2.3 Lova, LF+, and SuperNeo: precise lessons, not drop-in theorems

[Lova](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#lova), §1.2.5 and §§2.3–3, qmd `line:173-238`, `line:279-322`, `line:329-366`, `line:367-526`, strengthens the opening relation with an **integer** Gram matrix:

$$
AS\equiv T\pmod q,\qquad D=S^\top S\text{ over }\mathbb Z,
\qquad D_{ii}\leq\beta^2.
$$

Its recomposition checks $G^\top\widetilde D G=D$ and $\widetilde T G\equiv T$ preserve the original norm predicate rather than simply accepting an inverse-challenge norm loss. This is a design reference. The read paper uses unstructured SIS and a power-of-two modulus; substituting Akita prime-field/module maps and its nested proof order requires a new reduction.

[LatticeFold+](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#latticefoldplus), §§2.4, 4.1, 5–5.2, qmd `line:254-293`, `line:332-379`, `line:1018-1041`, `line:1164-1193`, defines perfect completeness over adversarially chosen valid instance–witness pairs. It folds a bounded relation and explicitly decomposes back to its bounded accumulated relation. Importantly, §4.1 itself permits relaxed openings $a=a'/s$ and noncanonical double-commitment digits: `split` is injective, but `pow` is not. Thus LF+ is not evidence that every algebraic opening is automatically an exact short source.

[SuperNeo](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#superneo), §6, Definitions 17–18 and Theorem 12, qmd `line:855-936`, composes a strong reduction with a weak reduction **using the same public projection and the required uniqueness condition**. §7.5, qmd `line:1117-1154`, explicitly decomposes the flattened representation to keep its norm bounded. Akita's own discussion, qmd `line:3991-4140` (also located by doc_grep at `line:4065`), says its folding/strong-check order is nested, not the sequential theorem applied verbatim. “Strong” in that knowledge framework is not a proof of (SC).

Recent primary research [Osadnik, ePrint 2026/721](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/721), **abstract read only**, proposes a final $\ell_2$ checkpoint with random projections and exact shortening in LF+ composition. It reinforces that extraction-time norm restoration is a distinct obligation. No theorem or performance claim from that unread body is assumed here.

## 3. Preferred native accumulator domain

### 3.1 Freeze a complete profile

$P$ fixes field/ring/modulus/bases; exact $A/B/F$ and $D/H$ views; source and decoding maps; physical coefficient orders; $N_*=2^{\mu_*}$; maximum fresh groups and lengths; challenge supports/operators/samplers; all digit depths and alphabets; quotient route; row catalog; and all receiver padding positions. It also fixes application identifiers, session/count rules, and $T_{\max}$. The profile and input declarations are fixed before fresh challenges.

The public state is

$$
a=(P_{\mathrm{id}},t,\mathrm{epoch},C,r,v,h,\mathrm{boundary}),
$$

with one ordinary full-grid evaluation claim $\widetilde w(r)=v$. History/root/CPU-link validity is authenticated by the receipt mechanism, not by this norm certificate or $h$ alone.

Choose the receiver's **coefficient packing** source map with $\delta_{\mathrm{com}}=1$, so $G=I$ on the source lane. This is a new admitted receiver shape, not a statement that arbitrary original Jolt handles already have identity source maps.

Let $\mathcal D_{b_*}=\{-b_*/2,\ldots,b_*/2-1\}$ for an even power-of-two $b_*$, with $b_*<q$. Every coordinate is serialized as a prescribed signed integer and embedded in $\mathbb F_q$. There are no extension-field-valued Boolean witness coordinates; if coefficients elsewhere use an extension, their base-field coordinate axes are separately recorded.

### 3.2 Injectivity is about all physical source cells

Define $\mathsf{Pack}_P$ by consecutive coefficients of $X^k$ in the fixed ring basis and consecutive source blocks. The receiver either chooses geometry with **no extra source cells beyond $N_*$**, or includes every additional physical cell in its domain certificate and requires the extra ones to be zero. In particular, a decoder that truncates unused positions is not injective on arbitrary physical sources. The preferred profile requires

$$
\mathsf{Dec}_P(\mathsf{Pack}_P(w))=w,
\qquad \mathsf{Pack}_P(\mathsf{Dec}_P(s))=s
\tag{ID}
$$

on all admitted physical sources. One convenient choice makes $N_*$ divisible by the complete source block coefficient width; otherwise explicit zero checks are compulsory.

Coefficient packing with (ID) is a coordinate permutation/grouping, so

$$
\|s\|_{\infty,\mathrm{coef}}=\|w\|_\infty\leq b_*/2,
\qquad \|s\|_{2,\mathrm{coef}}^2=\sum_jw_j^2\leq N_*(b_*/2)^2.
\tag{PB}
$$

For a single block of $m_A$ ring elements, use the corresponding $m_A d_A$ coordinates, not the energy of the whole vector. No extra factor is charged twice for a base-field coordinate already counted. Evaluation-trace encodings are not covered by this norm identity; an explicit expansion/operator bound is needed for them.

### 3.3 Exact relation

The witness is $\omega=(w,d)$, where $d$ contains **all commitment-side decommitment data**: ordered source blocks, inner-image digits, sliced outer inputs, all active $F$ intermediates/digits, and prescribed completion cells. Define

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,a;w,d)=1\iff{}&
\mathsf{TypedState}_P(a)\ \land\ |w|=N_*\ \land\ w\in\mathcal D_{b_*}^{N_*}\\
&\land\ \mathsf{ReservedZero}_P(w,d)\\
&\land\ s(d)=\mathsf{Pack}_P(w)\\
&\land\ \mathsf{Rel}_{\mathrm{com}}^{\mathrm{Akita}}(\mathsf{pp},C,w,d)=1\\
&\land\ \widetilde w(r)=v.
\end{aligned}
\tag{RA}
$$

`ReservedZero` enforces profile-required source completion, alignment, and tail zeros; it does **not** add unspecified “honest-padding” assumptions. The domain permits arbitrary alphabet-valid values in nonreserved live cells. It need not recognize whether $w$ came from an earlier Equation (109) witness. Historical relation checks are a separate receipt obligation.

$\mathsf{Rel}_{\mathrm{com}}$ means the actual Definition 10.13 chain ending at the actual $C$, not a fresh commitment to $w$. In particular, check

$$
A s_i=G_{b_1,n_A}\hat t_i,
$$

all declared $B$ slices, all active $F$ recompositions/matrix equations, their exact public endpoint, and every specified image/compression digit range. **Noncanonical $\hat t$ or $F$ digits are allowed** if these equations and alphabets hold. Their canonicalization could change an outer commitment; update must use the supplied chain. Identity source packing fixes $s$ from $w$, but does not canonicalize the rest of $d$.

### 3.4 Input relation at ORIGINAL handles

For a bounded batch of original groups, $\xi$ contains each decoded full-grid source and its commitment-side state. Set

$$
R_{\mathrm{in}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,u;\xi)=
\mathsf{TypedBatch}_P(u)
\land\mathsf{Frontend}_P(u;\xi)
\land\bigwedge_g\left[
\mathsf{Rel}_{\mathrm{batch},g}^{\mathrm{Akita}}\land
\mathsf{SourceDomain}_{P,g}(s_g)
\right].
\tag{RI}
$$

Here $\mathsf{Frontend}$ denotes the exact application's already-required PIOP/VM/statement predicate, not invented verifier residuals. The complete frontend and linkage are specified by the execution/receipt obligations. Every original commitment identifier and source decoder is preserved.

`SourceDomain` must be explicit and checkable: for example, bounds $\|s_{g,a}\|_\infty\leq B_{g,a}$ and $\|s_{g,a}\|_2^2\leq E_{g,a}$, optionally the exact canonical-source predicate. A range bound on decoded $f$ only transfers to $s$ if its source map has a proved inverse bound; (ID) is the preferred special case for the receiver, not a blanket property of original sources.

There are two input contracts, and they must not be conflated:

- **Bounded-source native input:** (RI). Prover has source witnesses/hints. A source-domain proof/revelation is linked to the same original handle; an application one-hot guarantee can tighten bounds only if it implies the exact source occupancy used by the bound.
- **Unrestricted Akita algebraic input:** Definition 10.13/Equation (202), or weak Definition 10.14. This does not imply (RI). The desired cheap bounded-source update is **not proved complete for that larger input relation**. A certificate is a genuine strengthened admission interface and must be integrated into the frontend/receipt, not quietly inferred from a verified Akita proof.

A decoded one-hot vector does not itself make a noncanonical multi-plane source a canonical one-hot source. Dense worst-case source envelopes are preferred unless the stronger same-handle source predicate is established. Alternatively one can cover the entire centered field source domain by $B=(q-1)/2$, with physical dimensions counted; this covers every algebraic source but will generally enlarge digit depths/security widths dramatically. Feasibility and useful SIS parameters for that option are not established.

## 4. Preferred complete final domain certificate: reveal once

A one-time, fixed-size **final** certificate is $\gamma=(w,d)$ for (RA). $\mathsf{Dec}_{\mathrm{reveal}}(P,a,\gamma)$ performs the following deterministic checks:

1. Decode the profile and state canonically. Check exact lengths, ordering, full-grid point arity, field encodings, time/epoch types, and registered matrix views. Reject unsupported profiles rather than selecting parameters from the witness.
2. Read all $N_*$ signed coordinates; enforce $\mathcal D_{b_*}$ and every reserved zero. Read all physical source cells in $d$ and enforce $s=\mathsf{Pack}_P(w)$, including completion cells.
3. Check every image/compression digit alphabet and completion zero. Recompose digits with the recorded radices; evaluate every $A$, sliced $B$, and active $F$ equation in the prescribed ring, ending at the original public commitment $C$. No $D/H$ opening path is needed merely to establish a commitment-side source.
4. Compute the full-grid multilinear evaluation $\widetilde w(r)$ and compare with $v$. Check optional source energy integers directly if the profile chooses a stronger bound than (PB).
5. Accept iff every check passes. Combine this result with the receipt/application's final validity predicate; this certificate alone does not prove history or halt.

Thus, unconditionally and for arbitrary supplied states/certificates,

$$
\mathsf{Dec}_{\mathrm{reveal}}(P,a,(w,d))=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,a;w,d)=1.
\tag{DC}
$$

This solves the domain-decider mismatch exactly. It does not rely on a probabilistic PCS evaluation certificate to establish the range. It cannot accept a small unrelated recommitment instead of a bounded opening of $C$.

The native running state is still one ordinary opening plus fixed-shape hints; the final **discharged** object contains a fixed-size revelation. Size is independent of the history length, not necessarily small in $N_*$. This is compatible with clear-only proof deployments, but supplies **no zero knowledge** and may reveal private trace-derived information. It is an explicitly conservative disclosure tradeoff, not a compact private-final-receipt solution.

An imported predecessor must have an available valid $(w,d)$ (or a sound linked certificate and available corresponding witness) before private update. The certificate need only be published at discharge; the universal relation proof applies to any valid supplied private witness, regardless of whether it was previously published.

## 5. Honest update and deterministic admission

### 5.1 Update witness algorithm

For a state satisfying (RA) and fresh inputs satisfying (RI):

1. **Public admission and binding.** Validate the frozen receiving profile and batch shape, count/index and application link. Bind $P$, the complete old state, every ORIGINAL handle/point/projection/claim, and input statement before new random challenges. Do not resize a response alphabet or receiver after seeing a challenge.
2. **Use supplied source states.** Take $s_{\mathrm{old}}=\mathsf{Pack}_P(w)$ and the supplied original-group $s_g$. Retain the supplied inner-image and $B/F$ digit chains. Do not call Commit to replace an input handle, and do not “repair” noncanonical chains by recomputing a different commitment.
3. **Prepare partials.** Compute the selected opening-method partial evaluations exactly from each $G_{b_g,M_g}s_{g,c,i}$. Canonically decompose new partials with the declared full-field digit rule. Construct the ordered shared $D/H$ payload from these actual partials. Source algebra and the true input evaluations imply the scalar opening identity. Bind the payload before its batching/folding challenges, in the paper's order.
4. **Fold.** For each admitted group/response, form
   $$z_g=\sum_{a\in\mathcal Q_g}c_a^A s_{g,a}.$$
   The coefficient-packed challenge injection and any extension coefficient axes are those in $P$. Decompose the reconstructed response in the **honest** response base and depth, which satisfy Section 5.2. Do not use the wider verifier alphabet to justify a shorter honest decomposition.
5. **Assemble the exact next witness.** Include the ordered $[\hat z_g\mid\hat e_g\mid\hat t_g]$ segments; quotient digits if the declared route has them; then the $F/H$ binary digits and each stage's quotient digits. Insert every scheduled alignment, ring-completion, and Boolean-domain suffix zero from Equation (109). Use the supplied $\hat t/F$ chain where it is an input-chain opening. Newly created $D/H$ and receiver chains can be canonical.
6. **Commit to the successor.** Pad to exactly $N_*$, using the already-certified length bound; commit under the frozen identity receiver profile, obtaining $C'$ and its actual canonical commitment-side hint $d'$. This fresh successor commitment is permitted: it does not replace any old/original handle.
7. **Range/relation reduction.** Execute the exact range, compression-binary, optional integer-response-energy, ring-reduction, and fused relation checks. All claimed values are computed from the assembled witness. The verifier derives $r',v',C'$ and metadata from the authenticated prefix, rather than trusting a prover-supplied successor descriptor. Output $\omega'=(w',d')$ and the prefix proof $\pi$.

All input-chain equations needed by the fold hold for **any** valid noncanonical $d$, because they are precisely the defining algebraic equations. Any stronger preprocessing routine that works only for canonical hints must be generalized to this algorithm or its input relation must explicitly require canonicality. No current adapter implementation is claimed to expose this interface.

### 5.2 Envelopes covering every relation-valid source

For each source summand use a bound guaranteed by (RA)/(RI):

$$
\|s_a\|_{\infty,\mathrm{coef}}\leq B_a,
\qquad \|s_a\|_{2,\mathrm{coef}}^2\leq E_a.
$$

For every accepted challenge require certified bounds

$$
\|c_a^A\|_{1,\mathrm{coef}}\leq K_{1,a},
\qquad \|\mathsf{Mul}(c_a^A)\|_{2\to2}\leq\Gamma_a.
$$

For negacyclic coefficient convolution, Young/triangle inequalities give

$$
\|z_g\|_\infty\leq U_g:=\sum_aK_{1,a}B_a,
\quad
\|z_g\|_2^2\leq D_g:=\left(\sum_a\Gamma_a\sqrt{E_a}\right)^2.
\tag{ENV}
$$

For sparse sign challenges with at most $h_a$ nonzero $\pm1$ coefficients in the actual injected ambient ring, $K_{1,a}\leq h_a$; absent a tighter certified operator filter one may use $\Gamma_a\leq K_{1,a}$. For uniform old source blocks, $U_{\mathrm{old}}\leq B_{\mathrm{old}}\sum_aK_{1,a}$, with $B_{\mathrm{old}}=b_*/2$. Challenges are accounted for per response summand, including claim/block/group indexing, not per nominal segment count.

For response radix $b_z\geq4$, choose the least $\delta_f$ with

$$
U_g\leq T_f(b_z,\delta_f)
=\left(\frac{b_z}{2}-1\right)\frac{b_z^{\delta_f}-1}{b_z-1}.
\tag{ENC}
$$

If the Euclidean route is selected, choose $S_{\max,g}\geq D_g$. Every successfully sampled challenge then admits every response. Coefficient centering modulo $q$ can only reduce absolute values/energy, so (ENV) also covers centered responses. The successor's individual digits are in $\mathcal D_{b_z}\subseteq\mathcal D_{b_*}$ if $b_z\leq b_*$. All other carried bases must similarly fit $b_*$. Binary $F/H$ digits are $\{-1,0\}$.

**Base-two edge case:** $\mathcal D_2=\{-1,0\}$ has $T_f=0$ at every depth. A positive integer response $+1$ has no such unreduced expansion. Either use $b_z\geq4$ for symmetric integer response coverage or explicitly use the full-field negative-lift rule and provision its longer depth. Silently using $\lceil\log_2(U_g)\rceil$ balanced planes does not solve this.

New partial/image/quotient field coordinates can always use Akita §3.3's exact full-field canonical decomposition with depth $\lceil\log_bq\rceil$ and its prescribed asymmetric lift. This guarantees encodability of every residue, not necessarily the centered lift. If a profile uses a shorter auxiliary depth, it needs its own universal coefficient bound. For extension coefficients, apply the rule separately to every recorded base-field coordinate. Quotient-free routing avoids quotient segments but is not permission to omit other tails. An integer quotient variant additionally needs the actual row-convolution numerator bounds; a field-residue quotient variant uses the full-field digit provision. The profile must distinguish these semantics.

### 5.3 Completeness proof, including arbitrary old states

Take arbitrary witnesses satisfying (RA)/(RI). By (ID)/(PB) their old/source envelopes are valid without a history assumption. For each accepted challenge, (ENV)/(ENC) yields valid canonical response digits, and the optional energy bound holds. By construction all supplied old/original $A/B/F$ equations are true, all new partial and $D/H$ equations are true, and fold-evaluation identities follow from linearity. Ring reductions and sumchecks on these true relations have exact honest messages. Every generated digit and required zero fits the declared receiver domain; the capacity condition admits the full Equation (109) vector. The new canonical receiver commitment satisfies Definition 10.13, and the relation reduction's derived opening value is the true evaluation of $w'$. Consequently (RA) and (DC) hold for the successor. This proves (SC), with failure only at the explicitly identified sampling/factor/search events.

This argument neither assumes old source canonicality beyond identity packing nor uses SIS binding. It applies even to a maliciously selected old state with an unusual but valid outer digit chain. Existence of many relation-valid witnesses would affect soundness/uniqueness, not this completeness proof.

## 6. Integer norms, no-wrap rules, and fixed-capacity costs

Norm predicates are over integers, not field residues. Direct final revelation computes the integer sum of squares and comparisons without relying on no-wrap field identities. It still requires injective alphabet embedding ($b_*<q$) and canonical signed encodings.

For a compact source/response energy proof using field arithmetic, reuse the precise Akita §6.2 conditions, qmd `line:2493-2632`:

- Direct norm route: with $n_z$ reconstructed response coordinates and digit-plane maxima $B_h$, require
  $$\max\left\{n_z\left(\sum_hb_z^hB_h\right)^2,S_{\max}\right\}<q.$$
  Then a field squared-norm equality is an integer equality.
- Digit-expanded route: fix a partition into segments $I_j$ with
  $$|I_j|B_hB_k<q/2$$
  for every digit pair. Lift each segment inner product uniquely to its centered integer; reconstruct all diagonal **and cross-plane** contributions with radix weights using integer arithmetic.
- Source Gram alternative: apply the analogous conditions to the actual source coordinates and same-handle source maps, not only folded-response digits. A field equality $D\equiv S^\top S$ alone is not an integer Gram certificate. Signed cross terms require centered lifting or bounded limb/carry arithmetic.

For instance, $6^2\equiv2\pmod{17}$ does not establish energy $2$ for source coefficient $6$. A claimed energy comparison in the field is meaningless without unique lifting.

The native receiver must satisfy, using these deterministic depths and all physical cells,

$$
\Phi_P(N_*,N_{\mathrm{fresh,max}},g_{\max},\delta_f,
\delta_{\mathrm{partial}},\delta_{\mathrm{quotient}},F/H,\mathrm{pads})\leq N_*.
\tag{CAP}
$$

This is the interface to the separate shape-closure obligation. Larger source/response bounds change digit depths, $A$ widths, tails, matrix inventory and SIS radii. No numerical feasible fixed point is proved in this document. A finite public profile is admitted only after (CAP) and its full security inventory are certified; “make $N_*$ large” is not a proof. Original root commitment geometry remains fixed when opening: changing the receiver does not change an ORIGINAL handle's source width or commitment map.

Final revelation transmits $N_*$ signed coefficients plus the fixed commitment-side digit chains. Check cost includes one full-grid MLE evaluation and all actual commitment-chain matrix applications. Prover updates scan the live vector and bounded fresh groups, build all exact successor auxiliaries, and execute the native reduction. These costs are independent of $t$ once $P$ is fixed; they may be large in $N_*$ and are not a demonstrated speedup. No extra ordinary evaluation proof is needed for the deterministic reveal decider.

With $a_h$ the actual successful challenge-tuple construction probability and $n_h$ distinct retries, deterministic response admission gives sampler exhaustion $(1-a_h)^{n_h}$. A conservative complete-chain budget is

$$
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{comp}}^{\leq T}
\leq T\left[
\sum_h(1-a_h)^{n_h}
+\sum_{h\in\mathrm{EvalRecv}^+}\frac{G_h(\kappa_h+m_{\max,h})}{|E_h|}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pow}}
\right],\quad T\leq T_{\max}.
$$

It is zero for the stated total-sampler, all-coefficient/identity-tensor, zero-grinding perfect-completeness variant. It is **not** a security-error estimate. Exact sampling, filtered-support densities, and the current backend's finite procedures must be reflected in $a_h$. Repeating failed samplers indefinitely may give probability-one termination but does not make a bounded-time procedure perfectly complete.

### Security ledger is separate from the admission ledger

For a native one-step reduction, the complete ledger must include original frontend/opening obligations, application batch separation, fold challenges, tensor reduction, range anchors and all range sumchecks, relation-row batching and ring reduction, source-domain projection matching, optional norm pair/binding checks, compression chains, setup-product/offloaded setup openings, and the final receipt. Some are already correlated pieces of an Akita fixed-schedule proof and must be counted using its actual combined expression, not summed again as independent errors. The proposed bounded-domain backward theorem adds obligations that are not yet assigned a proved error.

For a finite admitted family, a **conditional accounting template**, not an established native accumulation bound, is

$$
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{native}}^{\leq T}
\leq \varepsilon_{\mathrm{frontend/history}}
+\sum_{t<T}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{step},t}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{receipt}}
+\Delta_{\mathrm{setup}}
+\sum_{I\in\mathbb I_P}\mathsf{Adv}_{\mathrm{MSIS},I}.
$$

Each classical-ROM step term must itself charge its entire challenge/query ledger and any new certificate/extraction error. Setup statistical distance and shared matrix assumptions should be charged for the joint experiment; one may not assume independent fresh setups per step. The deterministic reveal contributes no probabilistic range/evaluation error, but commitment uniqueness and backward knowledge remain assumptions/theorems in this ledger.

Akita Corollary 10.34, Equations (230)–(231), qmd `line:4141-4250`, gives the classical ceiling

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{FS}}^{\mathrm{eff}}
=-\log_2((Q_{\max}+1)\varepsilon_{\mathrm{int}}),
\qquad
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{int}}\geq c/|E|
\ \Longrightarrow\
\lambda_{\mathrm{FS}}^{\mathrm{eff}}
\leq\log_2|E|-\log_2c-\log_2(Q_{\max}+1).
$$

The query count includes nonce/grinding probes. Thus enlarging only a public-residual accumulation field $K$ does not repair the original fp128 Akita error/query ceiling. Likewise widening native sumcheck fields requires an actual new admitted profile and adapter, not a claimed property of today's base-field-only implementation. The full profile must price successor compression/setup obligations before it is scored. The local source already labels the fixed-schedule knowledge theorem classical-ROM; quantum lattice attack-cost estimates do not make this accumulation, source-certificate, or receipt analysis quantum knowledge sound.

## 7. Certificate alternatives and conservative per-update refreshing

### A. Same-commitment full-grid range certificate

Prove the exact (RA) predicate without revealing $(w,d)$: range-check every full-grid coefficient, enforce all reserved zeros, and authenticate all $A/B/F$ equations to the actual $C$. The range proof and evaluation proof must use the same decoded source projection. Identity source packing is what turns full-grid coefficient range into old source shortness. For a canonical-source variant, explicitly prove canonical source/digit computation in addition to range.

This can be expressed as a finite arithmetic relation or adapted to the native range/sumcheck machinery, but a compact Akita integration must prove source projection matching and price all extra rows/commitments/challenges. The existing ordinary evaluation claim alone does not provide that certificate. Its completeness and knowledge/error costs are separate theorem obligations, not assumed here. A recursive proof of (RA) is also a certificate, but embedding it may restore expensive recursive verification and needs the receipt compiler's soundness.

### B. Lova-style exact source Gram checkpoint

Augment the source relation with integer $D=S^\top S$, bound each diagonal, and recomposition identities tied to the same original $A$ images and their actual $B/F$ endpoint. Use deterministic coefficient/energy limits and the no-wrap/limb rules above. For the receiver with one flattened source vector, a scalar exact energy is enough to bound its Euclidean norm; using it as a coefficient-alphabet substitute still needs per-coordinate range (or an explicitly changed domain).

The Gram algebra is valid; a compact prime-field/module-SIS protocol with matching Akita source extraction is **open**. Revealing only an unlinked $D$ or a new $AS$ does not authenticate the old commitment. Revealing $S$ and its full chain reduces to the preferred conservative method.

### C. Full-centered-field coverage instead of a stronger input predicate

Keep unrestricted algebraic sources and use worst-case $B_a=(q-1)/2$ in (ENV), with all hidden cells included. Provision full-field auxiliary digits and response admission accordingly. This avoids pretending weak extraction gives a short source. The algebraic coverage is possible in finite dimensions, but useful receiver capacity and collision-security parameters are unproved and may fail. It is an expensive fallback research route, not a demonstrated private native accumulation solution.

### D. Independently verified static certificate at every update

This is a distinct conservative alternative to **final-only** native accumulation. Every published state carries

$$
\beta_t:\quad
\exists(w_t,d_t)\ R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(P,a_t;w_t,d_t)=1.
\tag{BETA}
$$

The statement includes the actual $C_t$ and **its complete deferred evaluation claim** $(r_t,v_t)$, full-grid/source ranges, all required zeros, and the same $A/B/F$ chain. A bounds-only statement, or an ordinary evaluation proved about an unrelated bounded recommitment, is insufficient.

There are two realizations with different guarantees:

1. **Exact transparent refresh:** $\beta_t=(w_t,d_t)$ and verify with $\mathsf{Dec}_{\mathrm{reveal}}$ at every transition. Acceptance is literally equivalent to (RA), including the evaluation claim, for every supplied state. The prover can update any accepted predecessor with the witness contained in its certificate. Verifying the analogous fresh ORIGINAL-handle source/evaluation certificates and directly checking the exact local fold/Equation (109)/frontend/link relation gives a fully checked refreshing baseline without a hidden weak-to-bounded-source inference. This publishes fixed-size opening data each step, abandons privacy, and performs full state/bridge checks; it is not the proposed cheap private accumulator.
2. **Private static argument:** use an independently complete and extractable argument for the finite arithmetic relation (BETA). Its verifier runs to completion at each step and does not defer its evaluation back into the same unchecked accumulator. Its extractor needs no descendant accumulator extractor. This can hide $(w_t,d_t)$ if the chosen argument is zero knowledge, but that property and its security model must be proved for the actual compiler. The argument's error, work, and query costs enter the ledger. Argument acceptance certifies (RA) computationally, not as the unconditional logical equivalence (DC); (SC) still quantifies over actual valid instance–witness pairs, while the import of arbitrary accepting argument transcripts is charged to argument soundness/knowledge. Full revelation is the exact all-decider-valid realization if this distinction is unacceptable.

An update verifier must first authenticate the predecessor's complete $\beta_t$, bind it and all fresh input/domain certificates before its fold challenges, derive the actual successor $a_{t+1}$, and then independently verify $\beta_{t+1}$ at that exact statement. The successor statement may not be swapped after the static proof is made. Honest generation uses the actual $(w',d')$ from Section 5; hence the static prover has a valid witness for **any** (RA)-valid predecessor and (RI)-valid fresh batch. Its own completeness loss, if any, adds to $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{adm}}$.

**Fork-child endpoint rule.** Every accepting branch used by a local extractor must include an independently accepted $\beta_{\mathrm{child}}$ for that branch's actual $C',r',v'$. Different fork children may have different successor commitments/claims and therefore need different certificates. A final-only revelation for the published branch does not authenticate endpoints of other rewound branches. Independently extracting those static certificates supplies bounded authenticated output witnesses without calling a descendant accumulator extractor; static-proof oracle/runtime work must still be charged on all branches.

This fixes the *endpoint authentication* prerequisite for a finite local backward extraction. It does **not**, by itself, upgrade Akita's recovered weak predecessor/original sources to (RA)/(RI), provide same-projection matching, or give a polynomial multi-step extractor. For a completely conservative private construction, also prove an independently extractable exact local bridge relation containing bounded predecessor and ORIGINAL fresh openings, the real successor witness, the full Equation (109)/range/source-map checks, frontend/link checks, and (RA) at the output. That is the local bounded bridge contract of the extraction obligation, with auxiliary-input multi-instance extraction requirements preserved. The transparent realization can instead check those witnesses/equalities directly. One must not claim that merely attaching a range certificate proves the full bridge.

Per-update static proofs/revelations pay for a full fixed-$N_*$ state relation, including its evaluation, on every step. They can provide a conservative complete refreshing design but may reintroduce precisely the fixed-state verifier work that final-only native accumulation sought to defer. No speedup or compact receipt advantage is asserted. The final-only method in Section 4 remains useful and exact for domain discharge; it simply cannot replace the per-fork endpoint certificates in this conservative extraction architecture.

## 8. Failure cases and precise remaining gaps

1. **Norm-preserving-inversion fallacy.** In $\mathbb F_{101}$, $\Delta c=2$ and $\Delta z=1$ give $s=51\equiv-50$. The unit and product are small; the centered source is not. A bound on $\|\Delta c\,s\|$ cannot justify the $B_a$ used for honest re-entry. Akita weak binding prices cross-multiplied collisions instead.
2. **Concrete too-large admitted source.** A scalar algebraic opening with $s=-50$, $C=As$ and its true evaluation is valid for an unrestricted decommitment relation. With challenges $c\in\{-1,+1\}$ and a response encoder accepting only centered magnitudes below $50$, both honest responses have magnitude $50$. Admission fails with probability one. Thus a decider declaring this state valid but sizing the update for small digits does not have all-valid-state completeness. This illustrates the domain flaw; it is not asserted to be an attack on a particular Akita schedule.
3. **Noninjective gadget source map.** With two source planes, $G=[1\ b]$ has a kernel direction $(b,-1)$. Decoded-vector range alone does not bound arbitrary source planes. Such a change generally changes $As$ and therefore the original commitment; one cannot fix it by recommitting and claim same-handle consistency. The counterexample is to the norm implication, not a claimed cheap collision.
4. **Ignored physical padding.** If a decoder drops a physical coefficient, a vector can have tiny decoded range and a large hidden source coefficient. An honest zero-padding convention does not exclude it from Definition 10.13. Check it or include it in the complete grid.
5. **Canonicalizing outer digits.** Two radix representations can recompose to the same inner image but feed different $B/F$ inputs. Recomputing a canonical chain need not preserve $C$. Use valid supplied chains, or declare/prove a canonical relation.
6. **Wrong encoding envelope.** Verifier bound $\beta_f^{\mathrm{cert}}$ may exceed the positive canonical capacity $T_f$, especially when $b_z<b_*$. A security norm bound cannot establish honest digit encodability.
7. **Uniform-histories shortcut.** A Gaussian model or occupancy argument for generated canonical witnesses is not a bound for every (RA)-valid full-grid vector. (ENV) fixes that only if (CAP) and all response depths cover it.
8. **Final certificate does not prove predecessor shortness.** Even a genuinely bounded successor does not make weakly extracted predecessor sources bounded. A source-domain checkpoint/extractor must match the exact original/old commitment projection. This missing lemma is a soundness obstacle, not a completeness defect in the deterministic reveal decider.
9. **Fixed-profile feasibility.** No admitted numeric $(N_*,P)$ is produced here. If deterministic bounds cause $\Phi_P>N_*$ or invalidate SIS hardness, native re-entry remains blocked at the profile obligation. Redefining validity as “generated states that fit” is not a repair to (SC).

The necessary extraction contract is a local theorem of the form

$$
\mathsf{VerifyUpdate}=1\ \land\ R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(a';\omega')
\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf{ExtStep}\text{ returns }
\omega,\xi\text{ with }R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(a;\omega)
\land R_{\mathrm{in}}^{\mathrm{bd}}(u;\xi),
$$

up to the explicit knowledge/security failure and expected-polynomial-time budget. Existing Akita Theorem 10.23 returns weak openings under descendant certificates, not automatically this strengthened relation. Matching the bounded certificate to the extracted commitment-side source must be proved; comparing whole weak openings is wrong because partials and $D/H$ paths legitimately depend on the opening point. Long-depth extraction runtime is owned by the extraction obligation and is not solved by the final reveal.

## 9. Explicit comparison with public verifier-residual accumulation

For the conservative completed-proof route, fix exact typed linear lanes and split the original verifier as

$$
V_{\mathrm{full}}(u)=V_0(u)\land\bigwedge_\ell[A_\ell x_\ell(u)=y_\ell(u)].
$$

Its public accumulator is $(U_\ell,Y_\ell)_\ell$; its decider checks $A_\ell U_\ell=Y_\ell$. Given **any** old state accepted by that decider and any fully valid fresh input, the update

$$
U'_\ell=U_\ell+\alpha x_\ell,\qquad
Y'_\ell=Y_\ell+\alpha y_\ell
$$

has zero residual for every $\alpha$ by linearity. This is unconditional perfect arithmetic completeness over the complete public decider domain. There is no lattice-source norm, no inverse-challenge admission issue, and no private opening witness. Preserve $V_0$, all original bounds/canonical checks and application links; an input merely satisfying $V_0$ but failing its deferred equation is not a valid input for this completeness statement.

When the complete old state and fresh input are fixed before drawing independent $\alpha\leftarrow K^\times$, backward residual cancellation has probability at most $1/(|K|-1)$ in the elementary interactive argument; that is a **soundness** budget, not a completeness loss. Fiat–Shamir/receipt/QROM claims remain separate. Public state size is fixed by the lane catalog, not necessarily compact.

Native re-entry differs fundamentally: its decider domain must certify bounded private sources; its prover needs actual hints; sampler aborts can persist; and security requires extraction to the declared same-handle source relations. Public-residual completeness is a justified baseline, not evidence that the more ambitious private domain has been solved.

## 10. Integration contracts and evidence record

**Shape obligation:** receive $B_{\mathrm{old}}=b_*/2$ and per-block $E_{\mathrm{old}}=m_A d_A(b_*/2)^2$, actual challenge $K_1/\Gamma$ values, (ENC), every full-field auxiliary depth, physical injectivity/zero rules, and optional integer norm segment lengths. Count the exact Equation (109) vector and prove (CAP) after choosing those bounds.

**Commitment obligation:** preserve every ORIGINAL input handle, supplied noncanonical $A/B/F$ chain, exact decoder/full grid, and source projection. The final certificate checks the complete receiver chain and actual $C$, not a replacement commitment. Same-handle source/domain links are mandatory.

**Extraction obligation:** use relation names $R_{\mathrm{acc}}^{\mathrm{bd}}$, $R_{\mathrm{in}}^{\mathrm{bd}}$ and commitment-side projection $\mathsf{Src}$; prove a local bounded projection-matching extractor rather than recursively assuming predecessor validity or norm-preserving inversion. Computational uniqueness is separate from algebraic packing injectivity.

**Receipt/execution obligations:** authenticate all frontend, update, source-domain admission, and VM boundary checks and final (DC). Hash-chain membership alone does not supply these predicates. The reveal is one possible final discharge; compact final privacy is an additional requirement if desired.
