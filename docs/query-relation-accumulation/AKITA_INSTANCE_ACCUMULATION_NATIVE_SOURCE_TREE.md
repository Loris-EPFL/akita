# Native original-source extraction from a supplied accepting tree

## Status and scope

**A derived algebraic tree theorem, not an admitted concrete Fiat–Shamir theorem.** Independent review (`ReviewNativeSourceClosure`) found that the retained native domain checks permit noncircular original-source extraction. It also identified the exact remaining boundary: obtaining the required accepting tree from the actual chained H1610 compiler with a lawful, prefix-preserving full-answer sampler and paid resources.

The native certificate is a linear archive of one execution. It is **not** this counterfactual extraction tree. Neither a larger archive nor an optional wrapper supplies the missing tree-building theorem.

This note records the solution to the algebraic source-domain obligation. It does not approve numerical security, canonical commitment-state recovery, QROM security, performance parity or omission of the native Main Boolean prefix.

Independent final review (`ReviewNativeTreeNote`, confidence 0.92) found no actionable defect in this conditional theorem or its tree factorization. Its approval has the same limits above; it ran no builds, tests or numerical programs.

## 1. Conditional finite-tree theorem

It binds the complete original Main commitment $C$, source identity, A/B/F matrix views, shapes, decoder, raw injection and ordered application claim roster.

Assume:

1. approved prime $q$ and the degree-128 shell unit/difference law;
2. the actual approved source/path/row geometry and matrix occurrence bounds;
3. complete accepting descendants with the prescribed nested anchor/sumcheck/row trees and flat shell/affine families;
4. shared prefixes at each fork, including the shared D/H opening payload **within** a one-coordinate shell fork;
5. every native domain, normalization, reduction and terminal obligation, with the original signed/noncanonical RAM planes and both E2 planes retained.

A deterministic algebraic procedure on that supplied tree returns either:

- the original Main source $X$, its A/B/F algebraic decommitment/path data, occurrence-specific bounded weak-product certificates and authenticated application raw opening, with every physical coefficient of $X$ in $\{0,1\}$; or
- a nonzero kernel vector naming an actual matrix view, within its certified comparison/occurrence radius.

The procedure is polynomial in the **supplied tree size** and explicit source, matrix and witness lengths. No frontend Booleanity, Main118 correctness or initial Main shortness premise is used to establish Main bound one. Legal noncanonical paths remain possible: the result is not necessarily the honest canonical commitment state.

## 2. Backward induction

### 2.1 Terminal base

Terminal partials and predecessor-bound A images are fixed before the eight-coordinate shell bundle. For two accepting children differing only in coordinate $i$, subtract the A and both base-plane L equations:

$$
\rho_i=c_i-c'_i,\qquad r_i=Z-Z',\qquad S_i=\rho_i^{-1}r_i.
$$

The unit law makes this an algebraic source. The retained certificate, rather than $S_i$ itself, is bounded:

$$
\|\rho_i\|_1\le112,\qquad
\|r_i\|_\infty\le2\cdot7168=14336.
$$

The scalar partials-to-endpoint equation is checked directly. Competing sources against the same images yield a bounded A collision by cross multiplication. Division does **not** establish source shortness.

### 2.2 Authenticate one outgoing witness

For each fixed shell child, recursively authenticate the outgoing raw opening against that child's successor commitment. Compare successful extractions under the same complete commitment: obtain a common algebraic source $W$ or a matrix collision. Do not assume $W$ is bounded yet.

Reverse the fused degree-three sumcheck, its three independent coefficients and nested row vector. This forces exact A/L/B/D/F/H/scalar coefficient rows, virtual-table linkage, negative-binary spans and required zeros. Reverse the two degree-five product stages and their affine quarter combination. On the common source, the resulting identities enforce

$$
\prod_{k=0}^{15}\bigl(W(x)(W(x)+1)-k(k+1)\bigr)=0
$$

at every Boolean cell. Thus $W(x)\in\{-16,\ldots,15\}$; designated F/H spans lie in $\{-1,0\}$, and scheduled gaps vanish. These are verifier-derived alphabets, not honest-source observations.

The inspected native path uses exact quotient-free K coefficient rows; it adds no fictitious ring-reduction challenge. L rows retain both E2 planes and the raw-map adjoint. The admitted raw map preserves the ring-coordinate index and the same column permutation across blocks; arbitrary coefficient repacking would not preserve this argument.

### 2.3 Extract the incoming shell sources

Now the descendant-derived alphabets bound the response/path data. Compare branch paths against the fixed incoming B/F payloads and shared D/H payload; disagreement gives the corresponding short collision. Otherwise unrelated folding terms cancel in a one-coordinate pair, yielding each incoming source's exact A/L equations and bounded weak-product certificate.

Successor commitments may differ between shell children. Extract separately below each child before comparing the **incoming** sources. Do not invoke a sequential strong–weak theorem as if the successor were fixed.

Paper interfaces: [Akita, Definition 10.22 and Theorem 10.23](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita), lines 3951–3989.

### 2.4 Reverse repeated roots and receiver checks

After the three shrinking reductions, reverse final receiver normalization/domain, then the last update, then earlier updates. At each two-claim native normalization prefix, compare sources under the fixed receiver commitment; a three-child affine star with complete degree-two descendants separates its two scalar discrepancies. Reverse its balanced/binary/zero domain on that common receiver source.

There are exactly $T$ receiver prefixes: $T-1$ intermediate prefixes and one final prefix. The initial receiver is the installed canonical zero and has no receiver-domain prefix. Equal installed roles do not identify different receiver commitments.

### 2.5 Recover Main's native Boolean domain

Reverse the fresh Main two-claim normalization. It links the pending application raw opening and native Boolean endpoint to the **same** original Main source. Compare weak extractions across the domain children before interpolating:

$$
0=\sum_x\operatorname{eq}(a,x)X(x)(X(x)-1)
+\eta_0\sum_x\widetilde{[\operatorname{eq}(a,\cdot)Z]}(x)X(x).
$$

Separate $\eta_0$, then reverse the genuinely nested Boolean anchor tree. It forces $X(x)(X(x)-1)=0$ at every raw cell. The physical Main injection has 56 columns of 65,536 coefficients; its eight virtual selector columns are fixed zeros. Consequently all 3,670,016 **original physical** coefficients have bound one.

The selected native Main zero mask is empty. This proves Boolean boundedness, not the frontend's omitted-address/inactive-zero predicate. Those are different obligations.

## 3. Source comparison and opening-payload comparison differ

Across Main coefficient forks, the common object is $C$ and its source-comparison contract. Later points, commitments and D/H payloads may differ. First compare the original B/F paths. If they agree, compare original source blocks through

$$
\rho S=r,\quad\rho'S'=r',\quad
z=\rho'r-\rho r'=\rho\rho'(S-S').
$$

Different source projections and unit factors make $z\ne0$, while

$$
\|z\|_\infty\le K'B+KB'.
$$

No inverse-norm estimate is needed. Preserve actual integer lifts, source classes, cross-profile maxima and larger scheduled capacities. Three-/four-digit diagonal bounds are 7,339,808 and 234,880,800; terminal diagonal is 3,211,264. B/D diameter remains 31 and F/H diameter one.

**Within a shell fork**, D/H agreement is separately needed to cancel opening-side terms. **Across outer coefficient forks**, imposing D/H equality would incorrectly reject legitimate point-dependent openings. This distinction is [Akita, Lemma 10.15 versus Corollary 10.17](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita), lines 3745–3825.

## 5. Remaining concrete boundary

To obtain the actual $\mathcal N(\tau)$ contract, a tree builder must preserve:

- original selected prefix/query/index and original-execution success/failure;
- complete conditional answers, including rejected and unused bits;
- sequential bounded K/E2 and shell consumption;
- publication into chained history and all per-stream/global cutoffs;
- paid replay, auxiliary-query, expected-time and hard-cutoff costs.

[Akita, Lemma 10.2](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita), lines 3377–3423, provides separately indexed ideal coordinate streams, not a concrete H1610 seed-preimage algorithm or this chained compiler reduction. [Lemma 3.22](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita), lines 1497–1539, requires the complete conditional-answer law. Equations (225)–(228), lines 4143–4173, price the whole extractor rather than honest draw counts.

Therefore the algebraic source-domain circularity is resolved **conditional on a supplied tree**, but concrete compiler/tree-building and usable runtime-priced MSIS admission remain open. A wrapper checking only the same native verifier would inherit those inner obligations. The generic native Main Boolean prefix remains mandatory.
