# Small cyclotomic challenge units in the native profile

## Status and purpose

This is a derived mathematical argument for the existing native shell over the original degree-128 cyclotomic ring. It is not a complete accumulation soundness theorem. It does not justify replacing the selected bounded challenge distribution, dropping range checks, dividing lattice witnesses by field elements, or approving a numerical Module-SIS security level.

The actual sampler in `akita/crates/akita-transcript/src/native.rs::inner_shell` selects 40 distinct positions in 128 coefficients, assigns magnitude one to 24 positions and magnitude two to 16 positions, and assigns independent signs. Consequently

$$
\|c\|_1=24+2\cdot16=56,\qquad c\ne0.
$$

For distinct sampled polynomials $c,c'$, their integer coefficient difference satisfies

$$
0<\|c-c'\|_1\le112.
$$

The claim concerns the ring

$$
R_q=\mathbb F_q[X]/(X^{128}+1),\qquad
q=340282366920938463463374607427473266697.
$$

It assumes the approved prime modulus and the canonical signed-coefficient interpretation. The inequalities below were checked with exact integer arithmetic. They are not evidence about an unexamined alternate ring or field profile.

## Unit lemma

Let $d$ be a power of two, let $q$ be an odd prime, and let

$$
f=\operatorname{ord}_{2d}(q).
$$

For a nonzero integer polynomial $h$ of degree less than $d$, set $B=\|h\|_1$. If

$$
B^d<q^f,
$$

then $h$ is a unit in $\mathbb F_q[X]/(X^d+1)$.

**Proof.** Let $F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2d})$. Since $d$ is a power of two, $X^d+1=\Phi_{2d}(X)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$. Thus the nonzero polynomial $h$ represents a nonzero algebraic integer. Its field norm is a nonzero rational integer and obeys

$$
0<\left|N_{F/\mathbb Q}(h(\zeta_{2d}))\right|
=\prod_{a\in(\mathbb Z/(2d)\mathbb Z)^\times}|h(\zeta_{2d}^a)|
\le B^d.
$$

The odd prime $q$ is unramified in this cyclotomic field. Every prime ideal above $q$ has residue degree $f$, hence ideal norm $q^f$. If the reduction of $h$ were not a unit modulo $q$, it would belong to one of these prime ideals. That ideal would divide the principal ideal generated by $h$, so $q^f$ would divide the absolute field norm. Its norm would then be at least $q^f$, contradicting the bound. This proves the claim.

Equivalently, all irreducible factors of $\Phi_{2d}$ modulo $q$ have degree $f$. The ideal-norm argument supplies the quantitative reason a small nonzero coefficient polynomial cannot vanish in any one factor; nonzero alone would not suffice in a product ring.

## Exact selected-profile calculation

For $d=128$ the actual modulus satisfies

$$
q\equiv9\pmod{256},\qquad
\operatorname{ord}_{256}(q)=32.
$$

Exact integer comparisons give

$$
56^{128}<q^{32},\qquad112^{128}<q^{32}.
$$

Therefore every completed native shell draw is a unit in $R_q$. Every nonzero difference between two such draws is also a unit in $R_q$. An identical pair has zero difference and is excluded from the latter statement.

This is an algebraic statement about the completed shell support. Bounded rejection sampling can still abort. The compiler's stream/read caps and the conditional distribution after rejection require separate analysis; the unit argument does not remove them.

## Consequences and limits for Akita extraction

[Akita, Lemma 10.15 and Corollary 10.17](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#akita) compare bounded weak source openings under the same original commitment. Their A-matrix collision clears both weak folding factors rather than assuming their inverses preserve shortness. In schematic notation,

$$
z_A=\rho'(\rho s)-\rho(\rho' s').
$$

When the prescribed factors are units and $s\ne s'$, this vector is nonzero. Its norm must still be charged as the sum of two bounded products. Source-path disagreement instead gives B/F collisions at the certified cross-profile alphabet diameter. Opening-local D/H data and evaluation points may differ; they must not be substituted for equality of the original source projection.

The lemma above discharges only the unit property of the actual degree-128 shell elements and nonzero shell differences. Products of these units remain units. Transport to another source ring is valid only through the exact approved unital ring embedding; a packing map or coefficient reinterpretation is not automatically such an embedding. The implementation's complete weak-factor construction and occurrence mapping still need inspection before applying this argument to every recursive source class.

The result does **not** bound $\|c^{-1}\|$, $\|(c-c')^{-1}\|$, or extraction cost. It does not establish norm closure under arbitrary K-linear folding. These are precisely the distinctions needed when borrowing accumulation arguments from [Lova, §§1.2.1–1.2.4](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md#lova): invertibility and bounded witness extraction are separate obligations.

For the common-original-source refinement in [anchored batch extraction](AKITA_INSTANCE_ACCUMULATION_ANCHORED_BATCH_EXTRACTION.md), the remaining requirements are a complete bounded native source extractor, exact source-comparison classes and matrix views, collision norms under the certified alphabets, and complete runtime/query accounting. The generic Main Boolean prefix remains selected. This unit argument alone does not remove the current literal coefficient-tree factors or admit the protocol's numerical security.
