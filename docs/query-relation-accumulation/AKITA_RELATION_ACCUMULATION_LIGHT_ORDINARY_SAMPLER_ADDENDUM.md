# Sampler addendum to lighter ordinary extraction

This supplements `AKITA_RELATION_ACCUMULATION_LIGHT_ORDINARY_EXTRACTION.md`, with no protocol/code change or numerical admission.

## 1. Field interpolation is gone, so uniform field forks are not required

At a replaced nonlinear strong/tensor/batch/query/frontend node, the source probe does NOT prescribe a chosen decoded field value. It supplies a fresh full raw oracle answer drawn from the actual reference law, resets the absent future answers with that same law, and rejects until the exact root/acceptance/source gate succeeds. The renewal identity requires identical original/trial continuation laws, not uniformity of the decoded field element.

Therefore removing all corresponding field interpolation trees permits a more general local field law without changing transcript bytes. Uniform fields give the simple degree/K formulas in the main note. For a scalar field challenge c whose fresh conditional law D has maximum atom mu,

`Pr[nonzero degree-d residual vanishes at c] <= min(1,d*mu)`.

For independent coordinate draws with their actual fresh laws and individual degrees d_t, the same coefficient-induction argument gives the safe sum `sum_t d_t*mu_t`. This proof fixes the residual/source before the appropriate exposure. It does not apply when the adversary already knows future challenge coordinates before choosing their alleged prechallenge messages, or when the supplied raw wrapper does not have the claimed independence.

The source-probe hypothetical-source construction, original output retention, exact gate, weighted expected suffix count, and zero-success-prefix argument go through unchanged for D. The old-descendant-key factor is its actual point mass gamma_root, not 1/K by default.

Consequently there is no need to pay an unnecessarily coarse total-variation penalty merely to pretend a known nonuniform field challenge is uniform. Use the actual bad-set mass/maximum-atom bound. Sampler abort, changes in conditional laws and unsupported correlations remain separate issues.

## 2. Example from the retained policy, not an assertion about every Akita stage

`AKITA_INSTANCE_ACCUMULATION_FIELD_POLICY_COMPARISON.md:102–107` records that the inspected LEGACY FRONTEND reduces 16 random bytes modulo q and has maximum atom `2/2^128`. For that particular fresh scalar draw, a degree-d residual bound is therefore at most `2d/2^128`, and an old scalar guess has probability at most `2/2^128`.

This observation does NOT establish that Akita's strong-stage sampler uses the same law, that its extension coordinates are independent, or that an entire concrete transcript has the required indexed-key/reset interface. Those actual laws must be supplied. It is only an example showing how the lighter proof can respect existing nonuniform field bytes without silently demanding a different exact-uniform transcript profile.

The formal query protocol still uses its stated sampler. Under uniform a and sumcheck coordinates, its error is `(m-1+2n)/K`; under an admitted nonuniform variant, use `(m-1)*mu_a + 2*sum_t mu_t` with the actual fresh coordinate laws. No nonuniform variant is selected or implemented here.

## 3. What still needs exact conditional sampling

Retained UNIT-DIFFERENCE SHELL forks are different. The marked-original coordinate-wise game tests distinct accepted ring challenges from the admitted family and retains their full raw answers. Its published uniform-family/sampling and weighted support argument still needs the actual conditional full-answer law, including rejected candidates and unused bits, and the approved fixed-filter product family. The source-first replacement has not proved an extractor for an arbitrary biased/correlated shell law.

Similarly any field interpolation stage deliberately retained as a fallback still needs its actual fork compiler; its uniform-game theorem cannot be inferred solely from a maximum-atom ordinary soundness calculation. Removing a tree removes that field-fork requirement only when its full local source/point/ownership certificate is present.

No concrete SHAKE seed preimage operation is asserted. Ordinary fresh raw-answer sampling is simpler than prescribed decoded-answer sampling, but the retained shell compiler and full canonical/session-key law remain exactly the existing classical indexed-ROM obligations.
